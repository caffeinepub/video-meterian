# Specification

## Summary
**Goal:** Rebrand the site to "Odisha Exam", remove the stats section, and restructure course subjects by removing Science & Technology and adding General Studies (with sub-subjects), English, Computer, and Logical Reasoning.

**Planned changes:**
- Update site name to "Odisha Exam" and tagline to "Video Materials and PDFs" in Navbar, Footer, browser tab title, and hero area
- Remove the stats/counters section (500+, 50+, 20k+, 4.9 rating) from the HomePage
- Remove "Science" and "Technology" from all subject filters, category grids, navigation, sidebar, and Footer links across the frontend
- Add "General Studies" as a subject/category with five sub-subjects: History of Odisha, Geography of Odisha, History of India, Indian Geography, Indian Polity — shown in the subject grid, filters, sidebar, Footer, and Navbar
- Add "English", "Computer", and "Logical Reasoning" as standalone subjects (no sub-topics) in all relevant frontend areas
- Update backend category/subject data to remove Science & Technology and add General Studies, English, Computer, and Logical Reasoning so admin forms and filtering reflect the new list

**User-visible outcome:** The site displays as "Odisha Exam" with the updated tagline, no stats bar on the homepage, and a restructured subject catalog featuring General Studies (with sub-subjects), English, Computer, and Logical Reasoning — with Science and Technology fully removed throughout.
